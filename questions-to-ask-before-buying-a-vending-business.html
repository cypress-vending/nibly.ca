Questions to ask before buying a vending machine business

If you're evaluating a vending machine business, it's smart to ask a few pointed questions before you sign anything. Here are the questions we think a careful buyer should ask any vending opportunity, including ours, along with our answers.

Questions to ask any vending opportunity

Before you commit to any vending machine business, ask:

  • Does the company have a real, verifiable address and contact information, or only a form and a phone number?
  • Do you need a location secured before you buy, or will the company help you find one?
  • Is there an installation fee, and what exactly does it cover?
  • What are the actual specifications of the machine you're buying: size, capacity, payment options and monitoring features?
  • How long has the company been operating, and in what part of the vending industry?
  • Is the company easy to confuse with an unrelated business of a similar name, and if so, does it address that directly?

If an opportunity can't answer these clearly, treat that as a red flag.
