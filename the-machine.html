What Makes Nibly Next-Level?

48” Touchscreen Display

Nibly’s full-colour digital touchscreen makes picking snacks smooth and satisfying. Also fantastic for promotional offers and generating advertising revenue!

Remote Inventory Monitoring

Keep your machine stocked from afar. Nibly’s smart system tells you exactly what needs restocking, so you’re never out of your customers’ favourites.

Pay Your Way

No coins? No problem. Nibly Snacks vending machines accept tap payments from Visa, Mastercard, Interac, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Android Pay, and more.

Over 1,000 Snack Options

Before you buy

How do I get a package price?

Nibly offers Silver, Gold and Platinum packages. Contact the team for current pricing and an itemized breakdown of what your chosen package includes. Ask about the machine, optional locker, delivery and setup, and which services are included.

What ongoing costs should I ask about?

Ask the team to explain product stock, payment processing, connectivity, electricity, software, servicing and any location-related costs that would apply to your arrangement. Confirm amounts and responsibilities before choosing a package.

Can Nibly help me with a location?

Nibly provides location advice and helps buyers find businesses interested in hosting vending machines. Contact the team to discuss your city, available options and what is included in your package before purchasing.

What support should I confirm before purchasing?

The Nibly team provides start-up, menu and technology guidance. Ask about training, warranty terms, repairs, service coverage and response times for your location and package.
