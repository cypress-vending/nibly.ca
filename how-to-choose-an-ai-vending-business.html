How to choose an AI vending machine business

If you are comparing options before you invest in a vending machine business, look at the same set of questions each time: how location support works, what day to day management looks like, what the machine technology actually does, what support is available after you buy, and how clear the contract terms are.

This guide sets out those criteria and shows where Nibly fits against them. It does not rank companies against each other.

For background on what sets an AI vending machine apart from a traditional or smart vending machine, see What is AI vending?.

The types of companies you'll run into

When you research AI vending or vending machine businesses in Canada, you'll typically come across a few different kinds of companies. Some sell payment and telemetry technology that other operators build a vending business around. Others are local, independent vending operators working in a specific region.

Because these companies differ in what they actually sell (equipment, payment and telemetry technology, a franchise style opportunity, or a fully built business), check each company's own website directly for what it offers, what markets it serves and what a buyer receives for their investment, rather than relying on a summary.

This guide focuses on the criteria to use when you do that research, and on where Nibly fits.

Location help

Ask whether a company only sells you a machine, or whether it also helps you find a location if you do not already have one lined up.

Nibly helps buyers source a location if they do not already have one in mind, and also works with buyers who already have a location secured.

Machine technology

Ask what the machine can actually do day to day: how payments work, whether you can see inventory and sales remotely, and how much product it holds.

The Nibly machine is a digital touchscreen vending machine with a 48 inch touchscreen, cashless payments, remote inventory tracking and real time sales monitoring. It measures 40 by 30 by 72 inches (width by depth by height), offers 16 snack selections and 24 drink selections, and holds 160 to 480 items depending on configuration and product size. An optional smart locker is available for products that do not fit standard vending coils.

Management

Ask who is responsible for restocking, maintenance and day to day operation of the machine once it is placed, and how much of that falls to you versus what the company handles.

Put this question directly to any company you are evaluating, so you understand what your day to day involvement would look like before you commit.

Support and contract clarity

Ask what support looks like after the sale: how issues get resolved, and what is actually written into the agreement versus what is described verbally.

Nibly offers its machine through named package tiers, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Ask exactly what is included at each tier before you sign anything, since tier names alone do not tell you what is covered.
