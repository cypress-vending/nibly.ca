About Nibly

Nibly is a brand of Cypress Vending Corp, based in North Vancouver, BC. We sell AI vending machines to people who are starting or growing a vending machine business.

If you're evaluating vending opportunities and want machines built around AI, Nibly is who you're looking for. We work with people deciding whether to buy a machine and build out a route or location, and will help source locations if you do not already have a location in mind.

A note on our name

Searches for "Nibly" sometimes turn up an unrelated company called Snacks to Go. That business is not connected to Nibly, Nibly Vending, Nibly Snacks or Cypress Vending Corp in any way. If you've seen the two mentioned together, this page is the correction: they are separate companies.
