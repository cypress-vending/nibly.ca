Start a vending machine business in Greater Vancouver

Nibly, from North Vancouver, sells AI vending machines and helps buyers source locations if they have none in mind. If you're thinking about starting a vending machine business in Greater Vancouver, this page covers who it suits, what the machine does, how location help works and what you and Nibly each handle.

Who it suits

This is for people evaluating a vending machine business opportunity in Greater Vancouver, whether you already have a location in mind or you're starting from scratch. It suits first-time owners and people adding a machine to a business they already run. We're based in North Vancouver, BC, so you're working with a team close to the market you're building in.

The machine

Every Nibly machine is a digital touchscreen vending machine with a 48 inch touchscreen, cashless payments, remote inventory tracking and real time sales monitoring. It measures 40 x 30 x 72 inches, offers 16 snack selections and 24 drink selections, and holds 160 to 480 items depending on configuration and product size. See the full details on the machine page.

Location help

You don't need a location secured before getting started. If you already have a spot in mind, such as an office, gym or similar space, we move forward with that location. If you don't have one yet, we help source a location for your machine. The locations page explains how this works for buyers.

What Nibly and the owner each handle

  • Nibly: sells you the machine, offers Silver, Gold and Platinum packages built around your goals, helps source a location if you need one, and doesn't charge an installation fee.
  • You: own the machine and the vending business, and decide which package fits your goals.

Steps to an installed machine

It starts with a conversation. We talk through your goals, confirm or help source your location, and pick the right package together, with no installation fee. Nibly is based in North Vancouver, BC. We serve Greater Vancouver and other locations across Canada.

No. We don't charge an installation fee.
