What do you get from a vending opportunity vs a machine?

A machine bought on its own is just the machine, and finding a place for it is up to you. Nibly sells smart vending machines to people starting a vending business, with location help to find a spot. Anything else, such as training, supply or ongoing fees, isn't published, so ask about it in a sales consultation.

What's included when you buy into a vending machine business opportunity versus just buying a machine outright?

With Nibly, you get a smart vending machine with a 48-inch touchscreen and cashless payments. Remote monitoring shows you inventory and sales without a trip to the machine.

Nibly sells to people weighing a smart vending machine purchase, both those who will run machines themselves and investors. The test of any offer is the same for both. Compare machine with machine, then look hard at everything else on the table.

To compare a business opportunity with a plain machine purchase, check:

  • What the machine itself can do, feature by feature
  • Each extra the seller promises, listed one by one
  • Which of those extras appear in the written agreement
  • What happens if an extra you were promised doesn't arrive

You can look over the machine's features in full before you weigh it against anything else.

If I buy a vending machine on my own, do I have to find the location myself?

Not if you buy from Nibly. Helping buyers find a spot for their machine is part of what it sells. What matters next is how that help works, because it sets your timeline.

Before you rely on it, ask:

  • How does location help work, from first conversation to a chosen spot?
  • Is a placement guaranteed?
  • How long does it usually take from purchase to an installed machine?
  • What happens if the first location doesn't work out?

Get the answers before you pay, and keep them in writing. You can read more about how Nibly's location help works ahead of that conversation.

How do I tell if a vending business opportunity is legit and not a scam?

Start with who is behind the offer. A legitimate seller names its company and gives an address you can look up. Nibly is the smart vending machine brand of Cypress Vending Corp, at 204-370 Esplanade East, North Vancouver, BC V7L 1A4.

Then watch how the seller handles questions. A good one answers plainly, puts its promises in writing and gives you time to read them before any money changes hands. Pressure to pay fast, or vague answers, are reasons to slow down.

Before you pay anyone, check:

  • The legal company name behind the brand
  • A street address you can verify
  • A phone number and email that reach real people
  • A written list of everything the purchase includes
  • Where any figure you're shown comes from
