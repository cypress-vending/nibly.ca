A vending business with the location included

We sell AI vending machines, and if you don't have a location picked out yet, we help you find one. You don't need a location secured before you get started with us: if you already have a spot in mind, we move forward with it, and if you don't, we help source one for your machine.

What we handle, and what you handle

We handle the machine itself: a 48-inch touchscreen, cashless payments, remote inventory tracking and real-time sales monitoring, built into every Nibly vending machine. We don't charge an installation fee, and we help you find a location if you need one.

You handle the vending business built around that machine: choosing what to stock, setting your product mix, and keeping the machine running at your location. The remote inventory tracking tells you exactly what needs restocking, so you always know before you run low.

Restocking and support

Nibly's machines report sales and inventory data back to you in real time, so restocking decisions are based on what's actually selling rather than guesswork. As the owner, restocking the machine day to day is part of running your business. Questions about service and support for your specific package are best covered on a call with our team.

Steps to an installed machine

  1. Book a consultation to talk through your goals and your location situation.
  2. Choose the package that fits how you want to start: Silver, Gold or Platinum.
  3. If you have a location, we move forward with it. If you don't, we help source one for you.
  4. We deliver your machine with no installation fee.
  5. Stock your machine and launch your business, using the remote monitoring to guide restocking from day one.
