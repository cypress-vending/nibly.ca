Start a Nibly vending business

We sell AI vending machines to people who are starting or growing a vending machine business. If you're deciding whether to buy a machine, build out a route, or add another location to a business you already run, we work with you through every step, from your first call to an installed machine.

Who this is for

This is for anyone evaluating a vending machine business opportunity, whether you already have a location picked out or you're starting from scratch. We work with people deciding whether to buy a machine and build out a route or location, and we're based in North Vancouver, BC, serving Greater Vancouver and other locations across Canada.

What you get

Every Nibly machine is a digital touchscreen vending machine with a 48 inch touchscreen, cashless payments, remote inventory tracking and real time sales monitoring. It measures 40 x 30 x 72 inches, offers 16 snack selections and 24 drink selections, and holds 160 to 480 items depending on configuration and product size. If you need to stock products that don't fit standard vending coils, an optional smart locker is available.

We offer Silver, Gold and Platinum packages so you can pick the fit that matches your goals. We don't charge an installation fee.

How location help works

You don't need a location secured before getting started. If you already have a spot in mind, such as an office, gym or similar space, we move forward with that location right away. If you don't have one yet, we help source a location for your machine so you can get your vending business underway. Location sourcing is part of starting a Nibly vending business if you don't already have a location secured, and it's separate from our locations program for host businesses: as a buyer, you own the machine and the vending business, and we support you in placing it.

Questions to ask

When you're comparing a vending machine business opportunity, these are worth asking directly:

  • What's included in each package, and what's different between them?
  • Do I need a location before I buy a machine, or can you help me find one?
  • Is there an installation fee?
  • What does the machine track once it's running, and how do I see that information?
  • What happens if I want to add a smart locker or change my product mix later?

For more, see questions to ask before buying a vending machine business.

Frequently asked questions

What's included when I buy into a Nibly vending business, versus just buying a machine?

You get the machine itself, a package (Silver, Gold or Platinum) built around your goals, help securing a location if you need it, and no installation fee.

Do I need a location before I buy a vending machine, or does Nibly help with that?

You can start with or without a location already picked out. If you have one, we move forward with it. If you don't, we help source a location for you.

How do I get started with a Nibly vending machine business?

Book a consultation with our team. We'll talk through your goals, confirm or help source your location, and pick the right package together.

Do you charge an installation fee?

No. We don't charge an installation fee, and we help buyers find locations interested in vending machines.

Where does Nibly operate?

We're based in North Vancouver, BC, and serve Greater Vancouver and other locations across Canada.
