Hosting a Nibly machine

For workplaces and host businesses

Nibly’s host program places a fully stocked machine at your location with no placement fees. Remote monitoring informs the team when to restock. Share your location with the team to discuss whether it is a fit.

For machine buyers

Buying and operating a machine is a separate arrangement. Nibly offers business packages and location advice. Explore buying a machine and confirm package costs and responsibilities with the team.

Is hosting available in my area?

Nibly is based in North Vancouver and is seeking locations and partners in select cities across Canada. Contact the team with your city and workplace details to confirm local availability, installation requirements and who handles restocking and service at your site.
